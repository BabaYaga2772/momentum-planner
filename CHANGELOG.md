## [v0.1.0] — Mar 12, 2026

### Added (Initial Build by Claude)
- Daily planning (priorities, hourly schedule, tasks, notes, review)
- Weekly planning (last week review, priorities, life area goals)
- Monthly calendar (events, month goals, color-coded by life area)
- Habit tracking (boolean, quantity, timed) with streak tracking
- Mood tracking (5-level emoji picker)
- Gamification: XP system, 30 levels, 20 achievements, confetti animations
- AI coach chat interface (Claude API)
- AI insights: weekly analysis, mood correlation, goal suggestions
- Life Areas: Physical, Personal, Family, Work, Social, Others (customizable)
- Local-first storage via Dexie.js (IndexedDB)
- Deployed to Vercel at https://momentum-planner-nu.vercel.app
